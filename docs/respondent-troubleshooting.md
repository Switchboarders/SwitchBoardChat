# If your reply does not reach the visitor (for respondents)

Thank you for answering. Sometimes a technical fault on our side makes a reply disappear. **It is not your fault,
and you do not need to reset anything, sign out of everything or re-verify all your devices.**

## What you may see

- Your reply has a **red mark** in Element, or
- the room shows **"⚠️ The message from … was NOT received"**, or
- the visitor says nobody answered although you did.

## What to do (one minute)

1. **Tell us.** Write to `[CONTACT]` with the time and the device you used (phone or computer). That is all we
   need to find the cause.
2. **To keep helping right away:**
   - **Computer (Element Desktop / Web):** in that room type `/discardsession`, press Enter, then send your reply again.
   - **Phone (Element X):** the next chat opens in a new room and usually works. If it still fails, sign out and back in
     once on that phone, and tell us.
3. If the visitor is still waiting, a colleague can answer from another device: all respondents can see the new chat.

## What we will never ask you

- To reset your encryption identity or your recovery key.
- To sign out of all your devices or re-verify everything.
- To keep trying for a long time: if it fails twice, write to us and move on.

We fix the cause on the server and we tell you what happened.
