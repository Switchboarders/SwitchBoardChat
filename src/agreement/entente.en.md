VOLUNTEER CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (chat and text messages)

People who write to us share very personal things. Their anonymity and trust are essential. Quebec law (Law 25) also requires the organization to protect the personal information it receives.

1. What is confidential
Everything I learn while answering: what the person writes, their displayed first name, their phone number or any other detail that could identify them, and what I know about other volunteers and their conversations.

2. I agree to:
a) Never reveal this information to anyone, including family and friends and in meetings, unless the law requires it or to prevent a serious and imminent danger, following the organization's instructions.
b) Not copy, export, forward, photograph or screenshot a conversation.
c) Use only my own account, never lend it and never share my password.
d) Protect my devices (lock with a code, PIN or biometrics) and not leave a conversation where others can see it.
e) Not try to identify or contact a person outside the service.
f) Tell the person in charge of the protection of personal information right away if a device is lost or stolen, a message goes to the wrong place or a conversation may have been seen by someone else.

3. Duration
This commitment applies during my volunteering and after it, with no time limit. When I leave, my access is removed and I keep nothing.

4. Breach
A breach may lead to my access being removed. The organization may also have to report the incident to the Commission d'accès à l'information.

5. My own information
To prove my signature, the organization keeps my user ID, the name I type, the date and time, the device used, the version of this agreement and its digital fingerprint. They are used only for this proof and kept during my volunteering and for 3 years after.
